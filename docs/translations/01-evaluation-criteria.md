# Translation 1: Evaluation Criteria Must Match the Subject Matter

*From Challenges in Evaluating AI Systems (Anthropic, 2023) to the four-column weighting matrix*

**Research finding:** Evaluation criteria must be drawn from the internal standards of whatever is being evaluated. Generic capability metrics do not capture domain-specific failure modes.

**The Catholic version of this problem:** Catholic doctrine is not a flat list of equally certain claims. The Real Presence is defined dogma. Whether Limbo exists is a legitimate theological opinion. Whether a specific immigration policy aligns with Catholic social teaching is a prudential judgment. A benchmark that scores all three with the same metric weights is measuring something incoherent.

**The mechanism:** A four-column weighting matrix keyed to doctrinal authority level. Defined Dogma weights doctrinal precision at 0.30; Legitimate Theological Opinion drops it to 0.15 and raises stability and source citation to 0.20 each. The four columns come from Catholic ecclesiology. The research established that the column structure was necessary. The theological tradition supplied what the columns should contain.
